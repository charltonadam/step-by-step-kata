Steps

    Drag pieces to move · drag the round handle to rotate (Shift = free) · Q/E rotate · arrow keys nudge · Delete removes · drag empty space to pan · scroll to zoom · PageUp/PageDown change step. The faded footprints are the previous step.

    Step 1 · Mark the mat area

    1. Click Open projector window, move it onto the projector display, and press F (or its button) to make it fullscreen. Do this before marking corners.
    2. Click Re-mark corners, move the mouse onto the projected image, and click the four corners of the mat in this order: front-left, front-right, back-right, back-left. "Front" is the direction the students face.
    3. To fine-tune, drag a corner handle. Or click a corner and nudge it with the arrow keys (Shift moves 10 px at a time).

    Step 2 · Proportion and footprint size

    The floor now shows a grid of circles and the first step of the selected kata.

    Adjust until the circles on the floor are round, not ovals.

    Adjust until the footprints match your students' feet. Step distances, arrows and text all scale together.

    Step 3 · Start positions and colours

    On the projected image, click to add a start position, drag to move it, and right-click to remove it. Every student's footprints follow the same steps at the same time from their own start position.
